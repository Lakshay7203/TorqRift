# TorqRift — Original Arcade Audio Replacement Pack

All sounds are **new, procedurally synthesized for this pack**, not audio taken from CycloManiacs. Designed to feel closer to a playful bicycle stunt racer: bouncy ska/garage-rock beat, mechanical wheel/chain sounds, boing jump sounds, punchy landing and arcade reward jingles.

**Drop-in replacement:** These 21 WAV files intentionally use the exact same filenames as the earlier TorqRift audio pack. In your project replace all contents of `assets/audio/` with these WAV files, and make sure the active build folder contains the new copies. No changes to `AudioManager.h` or `.cpp` are required.

**Important:** CycloManiacs is a bicycle racing game, not a motorcycle-engine game. EngineIdle/Ride/Boost are now lighter rhythmic chain/wheel effects rather than aggressive synth engine tones. Keep `SetEngine` playback low if desired.

**Format:** 44.1kHz, 16-bit signed PCM WAV, stereo.

**Suggested mix:** SetMusic ~0.20, SetEngine ~0.15–0.25, SetAmbience ~0.08, one-shot SFX 0.45–0.70. Adjust by ear.

**No third-party audio, proprietary samples, or melodies have been used.** These are original sound effects and original music created for TorqRift and are suitable to modify, distribute, and include in your game.
